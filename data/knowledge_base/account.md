# NovaCart Accounts and Security

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-09-11
- **Company:** NovaCart
- **Document type:** Account support policy

## Account registration

Customers need either a verified email address or a verified phone number to create and access a NovaCart account. They should provide contact information they control and keep it current so order and security notifications can be received. Some account actions may require additional verification.

A customer should use one account for their own orders unless support confirms another arrangement. Sharing login credentials can expose order and personal information.

## Login and password reset

To reset a password:

1. Select the password-reset option on the NovaCart sign-in page.
2. Enter the registered email address or phone number.
3. Use the verification message sent by NovaCart to create a new password.
4. Sign in again and review recent account activity.

Reset links or codes should be used only by the account owner and should not be forwarded. NovaCart support will never ask the customer to provide a password or OTP.

## Email and phone verification

NovaCart may request verification of the registered email address or phone number when an account is created, contact information changes, or a security check is required. Customers should use the current verification message and request a new one if the previous message expires.

If a verification message does not arrive, check the spam or blocked-message folders, confirm that the entered contact information is correct, and wait briefly before requesting another message.

## Account security guidance

- Use a unique, difficult-to-guess password and do not reuse it on other services.
- Do not share passwords, OTPs, password-reset links, or verification codes.
- Sign out from shared devices and keep the device and browser updated.
- Review order activity after a suspected compromise.
- Contact support promptly if an unfamiliar order, contact detail, or password-reset request appears.

## Lost access to the registered email or phone

If a customer cannot access the registered email address or phone number, contact support at support@novacart.example. Support may request non-secret information to verify account ownership and may be unable to change the contact details until verification is complete.

Do not send a password, OTP, CVV, full card number, or an unmasked identity document unless a separately verified process explicitly requires it. An account recovery request is not approved until NovaCart confirms the outcome.

## Common questions

### Can support tell me my password?

No. Passwords are private and cannot be disclosed. Use the password-reset process.

### What if I no longer have my old phone number?

Contact support with the account email or other non-secret account details. Support will review ownership before changing a verified contact method.

### I received a password-reset message I did not request. What should I do?

Do not use or share the message. Change the password through the official sign-in page if you can access the account, review recent activity, and contact support if anything looks unfamiliar.

### Can I share an OTP with support to speed up recovery?

No. NovaCart support will never request an OTP.

## Important limitations

- Account changes may require verification before they take effect.
- Access cannot be restored solely because someone knows an order ID or email address.
- NovaCart cannot guarantee recovery when ownership cannot be established.

## When to escalate

Escalate suspected account takeover, an unfamiliar order or an unauthorized account, payment, or contact-detail change, repeated failed recovery attempts, a request to bypass verification, or any report that a password or OTP may have been disclosed.
