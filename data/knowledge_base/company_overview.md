- **Document type:** Customer support knowledge base

## About NovaCart

NovaCart is a fictional online shopping platform created for the AI Customer Support Agent portfolio project. It connects customers with a range of everyday products through an online storefront and provides order, delivery, payment, account, return, and refund support.

This document and the related policy documents contain fictional demo data. They are not the policies of a real company and should not be used as legal, financial, or commercial advice.

## Supported customer services

NovaCart support can help with:

- Order status, dispatch, tracking, cancellation, and eligible address changes.
- Standard and express delivery information.
- Return eligibility, return requests, damaged or defective items, and return shipping.
- Refund status and payment-related questions.
- Account registration, login, verification, and password resets.
- Payment failures and payments that remain pending.

## Support contact

- **Support email:** support@novacart.example
- **Support hours:** Monday-Saturday, 9:00 AM-6:00 PM IST
- Customers should include their order ID or account email when contacting support, but must not include passwords, OTPs, CVV values, or full card numbers.

## General policy principles

- Delivery estimates are business-day estimates and exclude public holidays.
- Tracking information is provided after an order is dispatched.
- Orders cannot be edited after confirmation. Address changes are considered only before dispatch and require verification.
- Cancellation is allowed before dispatch. A cancellation request after dispatch is not guaranteed.
- The standard return request window is 30 days from delivery.
- Refunds begin after the return is received, inspected, and approved. Approved refunds are processed in 5-7 business days, with additional bank or payment-provider delays possible.

## Important limitations

- Policies may vary by product category, payment provider, carrier, or verified order status.
- Delivery, cancellation, address-change, return, and refund outcomes are not confirmed until NovaCart reviews the specific order.
- Exceptions are reviewed individually and are not guaranteed.

## Privacy and security reminders

NovaCart support will never ask a customer to disclose a password, OTP, CVV, or full card number. Customers should use only the official support email domain, `novacart.example`, and should avoid sharing unnecessary personal or payment information in support messages.

## Common questions

### Is NovaCart a real company?

No. NovaCart is a fictional company used for a portfolio demonstration. All company, policy, and support information in this knowledge base is synthetic.

### How can I contact support?

Email support@novacart.example during the published support hours. Include only the information needed to locate the request, such as an order ID, without sharing secrets.

### Can support guarantee a delivery date?

No. Delivery windows are estimates. Remote-area service requirements, public holidays, courier events, and other operational conditions may add time.

## When to escalate

Escalate to a senior support reviewer when a request involves a suspected account takeover, possible payment fraud, a privacy or security concern, a repeated delivery failure, or an exception outside the documented policies.
