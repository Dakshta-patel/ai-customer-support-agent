# NovaCart Orders

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-09-11
- **Company:** NovaCart
- **Document type:** Order management policy

## Order status

Customers can track an order using its order ID. Common statuses include:

- **Confirmed:** The order was accepted and is being prepared.
- **Dispatched:** The shipment has left NovaCart's fulfillment process and tracking is available or being registered.
- **Delivered:** The carrier reports delivery.
- **Cancelled:** The order will not be fulfilled.
- **Return in progress:** A delivered item is being reviewed through the return process.

Customers should contact support with the order ID when an order status appears inconsistent or has not changed as expected.

## Cancellation

- Cancellation is allowed before dispatch.
- Cancellation after dispatch is not guaranteed because the shipment may already be with the carrier.
- A cancellation request is not confirmation that the order has been cancelled. Support must confirm the final status.
- If an item cannot be cancelled after dispatch, the customer may use the return process when the item is delivered and eligible.

## Editing an order

Orders cannot be edited after confirmation. Customers cannot add or remove products, change quantities, or switch variants on a confirmed order. A customer may place a new order if the required item or quantity is still available.

## Address changes

Address changes are considered only before dispatch and are subject to verification. A requested change is not effective until NovaCart confirms it. After dispatch, an address change is not guaranteed and may require carrier review.

## Common questions

### Can I cancel an order that has already shipped?

Cancellation is not guaranteed after dispatch. Contact support promptly; if cancellation is not possible, wait for delivery and check return eligibility.

### Can I change the size, color, or quantity after ordering?

No. Orders cannot be edited after confirmation. Place a new order when appropriate and request cancellation of the original order before dispatch.

### Where can I find my tracking details?

Tracking is provided after dispatch. Use the order ID when contacting support if tracking is missing or appears incorrect.

### What if I placed an order but payment was deducted and there is no confirmation?

Do not place repeated orders immediately. Check for an order confirmation and contact support with the payment time, amount, and transaction reference if available. Never send a full card number, CVV, password, or OTP.

## Important limitations

- Product availability may change before a new replacement order is placed.
- Cancellation, address changes, and status corrections are requests until confirmed by NovaCart.
- One order may contain items with different availability or dispatch timing; support should review the specific order record.

## When to escalate

Escalate when an order has a payment capture but no order record, duplicate orders appear, cancellation status is disputed, an address change may expose an order to delivery risk, or order records conflict across systems.
