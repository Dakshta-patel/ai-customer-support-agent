# NovaCart Refunds

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-09-11
- **Company:** NovaCart
- **Document type:** Refund policy

## When a refund begins

For a returned item, refund processing begins only after NovaCart receives, inspects, and approves the return. A return being delivered to NovaCart does not by itself mean that the refund has been approved.

For an order cancelled before dispatch, a captured payment enters the cancellation-refund process after the cancellation is confirmed. The cancellation confirmation, refund approval or initiation, and the time money appears in the customer's account are separate events. Support must confirm the specific status.

If payment was only authorized or is still pending when a pre-dispatch cancellation is confirmed, the authorization may be released or reversed instead of creating a separate refund. The payment provider or bank controls how quickly that release appears.

If an order has already been dispatched, cancellation is not guaranteed. When cancellation cannot be completed and the customer receives the item, the normal returns process may apply if the item is eligible.

## Refund timing

- **Processing time after approval:** 5-7 business days.
- **Payment-provider timing:** Banks and payment providers may take additional time to post the funds after NovaCart processes the refund.
- **Destination:** NovaCart normally uses the original payment method where possible.

The 5-7 business-day period begins after a return refund is approved or a cancellation refund is initiated, not when the customer submits a return request or when the parcel first enters transit. A bank or payment provider may need additional time before the processed refund appears in the customer's account. UPI and net-banking refunds may be subject to the payment provider's processing and the bank's posting time.

If the original payment method is closed, unavailable, or unable to receive the refund, NovaCart will contact the customer or provide an approved alternative process after verification. NovaCart will never request a full card number, CVV, password, or OTP.

## Refund status terms

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Return received | NovaCart has a delivery record for the returned item; inspection may still be pending. |
| Under inspection | The item and return conditions are being reviewed. |
| Approved | The return or adjustment was accepted and refund processing has started or is queued. |
| Processed | NovaCart has submitted the refund to the payment method. The provider may still be posting it. |
| Declined | The return or adjustment did not meet the applicable conditions. |

## Partial or adjusted refunds

A refund may be adjusted or declined when an item is outside the return window, used or incomplete without a qualifying defect, excluded by category rules, or returned without the required information. Support should explain the documented reason and escalate disputed cases for review.

## Common questions

### Has my refund been issued if my return was delivered?

Not necessarily. NovaCart must inspect and approve the return first.

### Why has my approved refund not appeared yet?

Approved refunds take 5-7 business days to process. After processing, the bank or payment provider may need additional time to post the funds.

### Where will the money be sent?

The original payment method is used where possible. If it is closed, unavailable, or unable to receive the refund, NovaCart will contact the customer or provide an approved alternative process after verification. Support may need to verify the order and payment status, but will never request a password, OTP, CVV, or full card number.

### What happens when a cancelled order has a pending payment?

If the payment was only authorized or remains pending, the authorization may be released or reversed instead of issuing a separate refund. The bank or payment provider may take additional time to show the released amount.

### What should I do if the refund is still missing after the stated time?

Contact support with the order ID and refund status. Do not share full payment credentials. Support can check whether the refund was processed and provide the relevant reference information where available.

## Important limitations

- A return refund cannot be promised before the returned item is inspected and approved; cancellation refunds follow the separate cancellation process described above.
- Bank, card-network, UPI, or other payment-provider delays may extend the time before funds appear.
- Refunds cannot normally be redirected to an unrelated payment method without an approved review.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a processed refund is not visible after the provider's expected posting period, the customer reports an unauthorized refund destination, the payment method has been closed, or the return inspection decision is disputed.
