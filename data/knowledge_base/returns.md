# NovaCart Returns

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-09-11
- **Company:** NovaCart
- **Document type:** Returns policy

## Return eligibility

Customers may request a return within 30 days from the delivery date. Unless the item arrived damaged or defective, the product must be unused, complete, and in its original condition with original packaging, included parts, and applicable tags or accessories.

A return request is not an automatic approval. NovaCart may request photographs, a description of the issue, packaging details, or other information before approving the request.

## Non-returnable and limited-return items

Some categories may be non-returnable or subject to special conditions for hygiene, safety, personalization, perishability, digital delivery, or other product-specific reasons. The product listing or support team will identify the applicable limitation. A damaged or defective item can still be reported for review even when the usual change-of-mind return condition does not apply.

## Return process

1. Contact support within 30 days of delivery with the order ID and item details.
2. Describe why the item is being returned and provide requested photographs or information.
3. Wait for return instructions and approval before sending the item.
4. Pack the item securely with its included components and follow the approved shipping instructions.
5. Keep the return shipment receipt or tracking record until the case is complete.
6. NovaCart inspects the returned item before approving the refund.

Customers should not send a return to an unapproved address. A return sent without instructions may take longer to identify or may not qualify for the expected handling process.

## Return shipping

- For damaged, defective, or incorrect items, NovaCart provides return-shipping instructions and covers the standard return shipping cost when the return is approved.
- For an ordinary change-of-mind return, the customer is responsible for return shipping unless the order qualifies for a stated promotion or exception.
- NovaCart does not reimburse unapproved premium shipping upgrades.
- Customers should contact support and receive instructions before shipping an item back.
- NovaCart is not responsible for an untracked return shipment that cannot be shown as delivered.

## Damaged or defective items

Report damage or a defect as soon as possible, preferably with clear photographs of the item, packaging, and shipping label. Do not discard the item or packaging before support completes its review unless keeping them is unsafe.

Do not include passwords, OTPs, CVVs, full card numbers, or unrelated personal information in uploaded photographs or messages. Redact sensitive information before submitting evidence.

## Common questions

### Can I return an item after 30 days?

The standard return window is 30 days from delivery. Requests outside that window require an exception review and are not guaranteed.

### Can I return an opened product?

Only when the item remains eligible under its category rules or when it arrived damaged or defective. A used or incomplete item may be declined for a change-of-mind return.

### Should I ship the item before approval?

No. Contact support first and wait for return instructions.

### What happens after NovaCart receives my return?

NovaCart inspects the item. If the return is approved, refund processing begins and normally takes 5-7 business days after approval, subject to bank or payment-provider delays.

## Important limitations

- Return approval depends on the item condition, category rules, timing, and information provided.
- Refund approval and delivery of money to the customer's account are separate events.
- Customers must use the approved return process and retain shipment evidence.

## When to escalate

Escalate when a damaged or defective claim is disputed, a return is lost after documented delivery, a customer requests an exception to the 30-day window, or category restrictions are unclear.
