# NovaCart Shipping and Delivery

- **Policy version:** 1.0
- **Effective date:** 2026-09-11
- **Company:** NovaCart
- **Document type:** Shipping policy

## Delivery options

| Service | Estimated delivery | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Standard delivery | 3-5 business days | Estimate begins after dispatch. |
| Express delivery | 1-2 business days | Available only for eligible products and locations. |

Delivery estimates exclude public holidays. Remote or difficult-to-service areas may require additional time. An estimate is not a guaranteed delivery appointment.

## Dispatch and tracking

- Customers receive tracking information after the order is dispatched.
- A tracking link may not show movement immediately after dispatch while the carrier registers the shipment.
- The most recent carrier scan is the best available delivery update.
- Customers should keep the delivery address and contact details accessible to the carrier where required for delivery coordination.

## Delivery exceptions

Delivery may take longer when an address is in a remote area, a public holiday interrupts the route, severe operational disruption occurs, the address cannot be verified, or a carrier attempts delivery unsuccessfully. NovaCart cannot promise a specific delivery date when an exception is active.

If tracking shows delivered but the parcel cannot be found, customers should first check with household members, building reception, or an authorized safe location. They should then contact support promptly with the order ID so the delivery record can be reviewed.

## Address and delivery changes

Address changes are considered only before dispatch and are subject to verification. After dispatch, NovaCart cannot guarantee that an address change or carrier redirect can be completed. Customers should not ask a carrier or support agent to send a package to an unverified location without review.

## Common questions

### When does the delivery estimate start?

The estimate starts after dispatch, not necessarily at the moment the order is placed.

### Why have I not received tracking?

Tracking is sent after dispatch. If the order should already have been dispatched and no tracking is available, contact support with the order ID.

### Does express delivery include weekends or public holidays?

The stated 1-2 business-day estimate excludes public holidays. Customers should not treat it as a calendar-day guarantee.

### What if my area is remote?

Remote areas may need additional time. Support can review the order's current status and available carrier information.

## Important limitations

- Delivery estimates may change after a carrier exception.
- NovaCart cannot control delays caused by public holidays, carrier operations, incomplete addresses, or events outside its reasonable operational control.
- A parcel marked delivered is not automatically eligible for a refund; the delivery record must be reviewed.

## When to escalate

Escalate when tracking has shown no movement beyond the expected window, a parcel is marked delivered but remains missing after the customer checks likely locations, repeated delivery attempts fail, or the address appears compromised.
